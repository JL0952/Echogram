# Echogram

Text your SillyTavern characters like they're on your phone.

<sub>A personal fork of <a href="https://github.com/mattjaybe/SillyTavern-EchoText">SillyTavern-EchoText</a> by mattjaybe.</sub>

</div>

## Features

A messaging companion for SillyTavern, where characters remember your story, text you on their own, and go about their days even when you're not talking.

### 💬 Messaging, your way

- A floating chat window alongside your roleplay, with Echogram, WeChat, and Instagram-inspired interfaces.
- Natural, multi-message replies with customizable texting styles and message lengths.
- Send photos, voice messages, stickers, songs, locations, files, links, and money transfers.
- React to messages, quote replies, and customize photos with your own images.
- Import your own sticker packs from ZIP files.
- Group chats, message swipes, saved conversations, custom themes, avatars, and fonts.
- Export a character's setup to a file and import it on another device.

### 🕒 Characters with lives of their own

- **They text first.** Characters can initiate conversations, send spontaneous photos or voice messages, and follow up without waiting for you.
- **They have schedules.** Give them weekly routines, errands, and daily activities. They might be busy, asleep, or simply unable to reply right away.
- **Time passes.** Characters keep track of what's happened recently, even when you haven't spoken in a while.
- **Their feelings evolve.** Rapport, Romance, Desire, mood, personality, morality, and texting habits can be configured manually or allowed to develop through interactions.

### 🔗 Connected to your roleplay

- Characters know your story's opening and can respond to events in your main SillyTavern chat.
- Spend time together in person through With You, with events and conversations carried between your roleplay and messaging.
- Messages and simulated daily activities pause appropriately while you're roleplaying together.
- Long conversations are summarized into memories, helping characters maintain continuity over time.

### 🎨 Make it yours

Choose a different messaging interface for each character, customize their appearance and behavior, and decide how much control you want over their lives.

Works on desktop and mobile.

## Install

In SillyTavern, open **Extensions → Install extension** and paste:

```
https://github.com/JL0952/Echogram
```
